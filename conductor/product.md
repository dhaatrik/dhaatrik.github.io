# Initial Concept

Personal website and engineering diary of Dhaatrik Chowdhury, documenting projects, pedagogical philosophy, and technical transmissions, built with Astro and Tailwind CSS v4 for extreme speed and a sci-fi mission-control aesthetic.

## Target Audience

- **Tech Recruiters & Engineering Leads**: Teams looking for a hands-on AI engineer and builder.
- **Fellow Developers & Students**: Peers and learners interested in first-principles engineering and open-source tooling.
- **Collaborators & Founders**: Technical operators seeking a co-builder.

## Primary Call to Action

- **Read Transmissions**: Explore honest technical deep dives, failure logs, and architectural trade-offs.
- **View Projects**: Audit the complete portfolio of built products, live sandboxes, and offline tools.
- **Personnel Dossier**: Review background, capabilities, and educational journey.

## Content Strategy

- **Mission Reports**: Six-part structured write-ups with concrete scope, stack tables, and explicit fuckups & learnings.
- **Pedagogy Logs**: First-principles reflections on intuitive physics, mathematics, and sibling-centric teaching.
- **Architecture Appendixes**: Unembellished technical deep dives into worker protocols, client-only state machines, and offline encryption.

## Future Expansion

- **Interactive Sandboxes**: Live trajectory and client-side simulation modules.
- **Pedagogy Tooling**: Open-source utilities for private tutors and independent educators.
